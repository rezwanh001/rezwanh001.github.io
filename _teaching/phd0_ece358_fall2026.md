    <h4><i class="fa-solid fa-link"></i> Course Description</h4>
    <a href="https://uwflow.com/course/ECE358" target="_blank">View on UWFlow</a><br><br>
    An introduction to computer networks and the Internet, organized around the
    layered network architecture. The course covers the application layer (HTTP,
    DNS, and network applications), the transport layer (UDP and TCP, reliable
    data transfer, flow and congestion control), the network layer (IP addressing,
    forwarding, and routing), and the link layer (multiple access, Ethernet, and
    switching), together with hands-on socket programming.<br>
    <hr>

    <h4><i class="fa-solid fa-diagram-project"></i> Topics Covered</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>Network architecture, protocol layering, and the Internet</li>
      <li>Application layer — HTTP, DNS, and network applications</li>
      <li>Transport layer — UDP, TCP, reliable delivery, and congestion control</li>
      <li>Network layer — IP, addressing, forwarding, and routing</li>
      <li>Link layer — multiple access, Ethernet, and switching</li>
      <li>Socket programming</li>
    </ul>
